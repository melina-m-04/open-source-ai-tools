# Scope: where your issue lives

## Where your issue lives

Any public repository, one issue at a time: the issue your claim
names. That issue comes to you through your own evidence trail, the
scout's saved read-out in `~/.claude/skills/issue-scout/scan-output.md`
(a site pick graded in direct mode or a scan's top five). Do not grade
drafts or packages for an issue your scout never graded. If the issue is
not in that file, stop and suggest running `issue-scout` in direct mode
on the issue URL first.

## Contribution rules for public repositories

On a public project, the people reading your comments are maintainers
and contributors you have never worked with, and there is no private
channel to coordinate through. Three rules follow:

- **Someone else's claim blocks you.** A claim comment or an assignee
  on an issue means someone got there first, and you have no channel
  to coordinate with them. Never claim over one; pick again. First
  claim stands, including yours: if someone claims after you, your
  claim holds, and if you stop working, release your claim honestly
  in the thread.
- **Never piggyback, and never duplicate.** If the thread already
  holds a reproduction, do not post "same here, can confirm". Add a
  comment only when it carries something new from your environment,
  in your words.
- **The repo's own rules outrank yours.** CONTRIBUTING.md and any
  stated contribution or AI policy are the standard you are held to
  whether you read them or not. When a stated ask conflicts with your
  usual habits, the repo wins; say so in your evidence if it changes
  what you do.
